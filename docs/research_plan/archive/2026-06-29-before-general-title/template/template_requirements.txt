Research plan template requirements extracted from the original DOC/TXT.

This file is for drafting only. Do not include these administrative notes in
the final PDF.

Required proposal fields:
- Title of the proposed research.
- Principal investigator name, position, and affiliated department.
- Collaborator names and departments, if any.
- Project duration, including proposed start date and scheduled completion date.
- Project abstract, no more than 150 words.
- Key objectives.
- Research methodology.
- Significance of the proposed research and major deliverables.
- Funding requested with budget breakdown and justification for each item.
- Ethics and safety statement for animals, human subjects, biological safety,
  chemical safety, and radiation safety where applicable.

Formatting and submission constraints:
- Proposal should be no more than 5 A4 pages.
- Budget table categories in the template:
  Staffing, Equipment, General expenses, Conference, Overseas Travel,
  Total Funding.
- Highlight unknown fields in drafts, but resolve them before formal submission
  if the PI requires a final clean version.
