# Separator pilot v1: dictionary and provenance

Authoritative data: `data/separators/dataset.json`; CSV views are regenerated by
`src/materials/separator_ingest.py`. The web app builds a SQLite export containing
`sources`, `records`, and `evidence`, with JSON bodies retaining nested conditions.

- A **source** is one publication or screened lead. Twelve leads include four
  extracted primary studies, five abstract-only leads and three earlier background
  leads. Background titles are descriptive labels; they are not extracted datasets.
- A **record** is one source/sample formulation identity. Twenty records include
  controls and one reported failed formulation. They are not twenty independent
  studies, batches, or twenty compatible numeric training samples.
- An **observation** is a reported endpoint for that formulation: numeric value or
  explicit categorical failure, unit, source evidence ID, qualifier and conditions.
  There are eighteen observations across the twenty records. Repeated temperatures
  or contact-angle error terms are not counted as additional formulations.
- **inputs** contain designed ingredients/process settings. Measured final
  thickness and contact angle belong to observations and cannot become inputs to
  a before-fabrication prediction. `null` means unknown/not applicable as explained
  by the record; it never means a measured zero.
- **evidence_ids** link recipe/material/test paragraphs to each record. Observation
  evidence is a subset of those IDs. XML paragraph indices are 1-based over all `p`
  elements. Table 2 uses its XML ID. Original text and exact downloaded-file SHA-256
  are checked on application startup.
- Loading uses `mg/cm²`, reported powder ratio, or reported `wt%` according to the
  source. Do not convert between these without area/mass/basis evidence. Where a
  ratio or weight-percent denominator is unspecified, the uncertainty stays visible.
- Conductivity is `mS/cm`. Yin's approximate `2 × 10⁻⁴` and `0.6 × 10⁻⁴ S/cm`
  become `0.2` and `0.06 mS/cm`; source precision remains approximate.
- The PP/BNNT ionic-conductivity measurement temperature is not explicitly stated
  in the extracted method. A user-specified temperature makes the numeric task
  unsupported. CA results at 65°C and solid-electrolyte results at 70°C cannot be
  merged into that PP cohort.
- CNF water contact angle is not electrolyte contact angle. CA applicator gaps of
  50/100/200 µm are not the measured final thicknesses of 88/110/129 µm.
- `split=train/test/context` is frozen before inference. The two train records are
  bare PP and raw-BNNT PP at 0.3 mg/cm². Purified-BNNT PP at 0.5 mg/cm² is the one
  unused application-level test record. Public-paper pretraining exposure cannot
  be excluded. This split supports only a functional within-study check.

Public source XML is retained under `official_docs/separators/`, with article
titles, authors, DOI and CC BY 4.0 notices intact. Normalized data and selected
passages are derived from those sources. No figures were interpreted, and no
private meeting or partner content is published. Abstract-only leads are links
and metadata, not numerical training labels.
