Research Plan Template
Suggested checklist of items to be included(Please confine your proposal to not more than 5 A4 pages)


Title of the proposed research

Name of the principal investigator, position and affiliated department

Name(s) of the collaborator(s) and their respective department(s), if any

Duration of the project (including proposed start date and scheduled completion date)

Abstract of the project in not more than 150 words
(If the application is funded, the abstract may be published on IRIS and the CityU Research Report as the project synopsis.)

Key objectives

Research methodology

Significance of the proposed research and major deliverables 

Funding requested with budget breakdown (staffing, general expenses, equipment, overseas travel and conference expenses) and justifications for each item

For projects involving living animals and/or human subjects, application for review are mandatory. For biological/chemical/radiation safety, PIs have to attach the approval documents from the respective safety officers with their project proposals. (please visit RO website at http://www.cityu.edu.hk/ro/studentlan/dlethic.htm in relation to these requirements and download application forms)
